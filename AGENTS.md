## 1. Project Identity & Mission
* **Project Name:** AI-Powered Wealth Mentor & Multi-Asset Intelligence
* **Repository:** [Anshhh0306/Finance](https://github.com/Anshhh0306/Finance)
* **Core Philosophy:** Moving away from passive, backward-looking expense trackers (like Mint/Excel). This app is an **active, lifelong AI money mentor and scenario simulator** that helps users grow their net worth across **Gold, Stocks, ETFs, and Cash**.

---

## 2. The 5 Core Product Pillars (Always Remember These)

1. **The Financial Twin (Adaptive AI Memory):**
   * Continuously learns the user's spending habits, money psychology, risk tolerance, and life goals through natural chat.
   * Proactively reaches out when extra savings or market opportunities occur.
2. **"What-If" Multi-Asset Scenario Simulator:**
   * Runs historical backtesting and projections comparing **Physical Gold, Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB), Equities/Index Funds, and Liquid Cash**.
   * Provides real-time opportunity cost "Sanity Checks" on discretionary purchases.
3. **Zero-Effort Automation (No Manual Entry):**
   * Solves the #1 reason finance apps fail (typing fatigue after 3 days).
   * Automatically ingests transactions via on-device SMS/push notifications, 1-click monthly CAS statement syncs, and 1-tap micro-prompts.
4. **Zero-Knowledge Privacy Vault:**
   * All personal financial records, statements, and balances are stored encrypted on the user's device.
   * PII is stripped out before any mathematical queries reach the AI engine.
5. **Gamified Discipline & Token System:**
   * Users earn habit reward tokens for savings streaks and budget discipline, which unlock institutional-grade stock valuation models.

---

## 3. The Lifelong Journey Architecture
* **20s:** Wealth foundation, emergency cushion, high-growth equity + gold hedge.
* **30s:** Big life milestones, home down payment simulation, child education fund.
* **40s–50s:** Wealth protection, tax harvesting, rebalancing out of volatility.
* **60s+:** Retirement cash flow, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), dividend income.
